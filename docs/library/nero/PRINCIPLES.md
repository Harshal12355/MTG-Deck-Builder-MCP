# Deckbuilding principles — NeRo (@_NeRo on Moxfield)

Self-described 'Dimir Degenerate' who builds high-power Commander decks at a ~$100 cap, aimed at $100 Budget Competitive and high-power Bracket 4 tables. Every list is tagged and the flagship ones carry primers. Co-builds with Navonod; associated with Budget Pub Stomper and the Stomping Grounds MTG Discord.

Distilled from his primers and lists (paraphrased). Also served by the `reference_principles` MCP tool.

## 1. Build the whole deck around what the commander actually breaks

Read the commander for the rules it bends, not its surface theme. Vren is a removal deck that happens to make Rats, not Rat tribal. Tannuk is a two-lands-a-turn value engine and combo outlet, not a landfall token deck. Ratadrabik's real text is 'the Ring makes tokens legendary again'. Hidetsugu and Kairi is a pile of clones, not a combo deck.

*Seen in:* Vren, Tannuk, Ratadrabik, Hidetsugu and Kairi, Meria

## 2. Every card must pass a narrow inclusion test

Write down the 3-4 jobs a card can do and cut anything that does none of them. Kefka's test: (1) ramp the commander out fast, (2) maximize commander triggers, (3) win or tutor for the win, (4) interaction. Prefer cards that do two jobs (Skeleton Key and Call of the Ring are draw plus evasion; Bridgeworks Battle and Stump Stomp are lands plus removal; Archdruid's Charm is a tutor, ramp and removal).

*Seen in:* Kefka, Hapatra, Tannuk

## 3. Layered, interwoven combos instead of one expensive 2-card win

Without Thassa's Oracle / Breach money, build many overlapping 2-4 card lines where every piece is good on its own: 'for every A, 2-3 Bs and vice versa'. Interchangeable halves (Blood Artist / Zulaport / Obelisk Spider; Combat Celebrant / Fear of Missing Out; Electroduplicate / Twinflame) mean tutors and natural draws both assemble wins and few cards are ever dead. Ideally most wins work without the commander.

*Seen in:* Kefka, Hapatra, Tannuk, Ratadrabik, Alaundo, Meria

## 4. Consistency from density first, tutors second

Run high counts of each core effect so the plan happens without tutors (Vren runs zero tutors and still locks games via edict density). When tutors are in, use budget ones that put the piece into play or on top: Demonic Counsel ('a Demonic Tutor at a 40th of the price'), Insatiable Avarice, Lively Dirge, Wishclaw Talisman, Case of the Stashed Skeleton, Dimir Machinations / Shred Memory (transmute), Chord of Calling, Eldritch Evolution, Shared Summons (grabs both halves at once), Gifts Ungiven piles.

*Seen in:* Vren, Vohar, Hapatra, Tannuk, Kefka

## 5. Very heavy, very cheap interaction, much of it free or instant

About 20-25 interaction slots in control shells (Vohar: 25). Lean on 1-mana and free answers: Spell Pierce, Dispel, Miscast, Siren Stormtamer, An Offer You Can't Refuse, Malevolent Hermit, Bolt Bend, Commandeer / Misdirection / Flare of Duplication (free), Rapid Hybridization, Pongify, Lightning Bolt, Ghostfire Slice. Off-color 'counters' in green (Autumn's Veil, Imp's Mischief, Tyvar's Stand, Veil of Summer) protect the combo. Redirect effects (Bolt Bend, Return the Favor, Ricochet Trap) are protection and removal in one.

*Seen in:* Kefka, Vohar, Vren, Tannuk, Hapatra

## 6. Protect the commander and combo, and punish the answer

Blink the commander in response to removal (Essence Flux, Ghostly Flicker) so the answer fizzles and its ETB triggers again. Snapback works as an emergency 'abort'. Abolisher-style permanents (Conqueror's Flail, Destiny Spinner) stop opponents answering your combo turn. Sylvan Safekeeper, Welding Jar and Herbal Poultice are reusable protection that also fuel combos.

*Seen in:* Kefka, Hapatra, Tannuk, Meria

## 7. Mulligan for the commander-on-curve hand

Pick the target turn for the commander and mulligan hard toward it. Kefka: keep only 2-3 lands plus turn-1 ramp and turn-2 ramp, or 2-3 lands plus a 2-mana producer (Generator Servant gives turn-3 Kefka with haste). Expect 0-3 mulligans. Codie: 2-3 lands, a turn-2 Codie, 4 mana on turn 3 and two untap spells. Bottom free-cast combo pieces you draw. But hold the commander a turn if casting it later lets you protect it.

*Seen in:* Kefka, Gateway to Glory (Codie), Hapatra, Meria

## 8. Ramp is about the commander's turn, not big mana

Budget fast mana means Dark Ritual, Rite of Flame, Simian / Elvish Spirit Guide, Springleaf Drum, 1-mana dorks and 2-mana rocks (Talismans, Signets, Fellwar Stone, Arcane Signet, Pentad Prism). Rituals double as storm fuel and as mana for combo turns. Use creature-based ramp that also fuels the plan (Orcish Lumberjack, Tinder Wall, Channeler Initiate, Devoted Druid, Cryptolith Rite). If the commander makes mana (Meria), skip rocks.

*Seen in:* Kefka, Hapatra, Meria, Alaundo

## 9. Draw engines that feed off the deck's own actions

Prefer repeatable engines tied to the plan over one-shot draw: Skullclamp, Braids Arisen Nightmare, Morbid Opportunist, Toski and Grazilaxx (with tokens), Enduring Curiosity, Idol of Oblivion, Talion ('just name 2'), Mystic Remora, Dark Confidant, Keen Sense / Snake Umbra on the commander, utility lands that draw (Cephalid Coliseum, Fiery Islet). Even weak-looking draw (sorcery-speed draw-3s) gets good if the commander cheats timing or cost.

*Seen in:* Vren, Hapatra, Vohar, Tannuk, Alaundo, Kefka

## 10. Run stax you can break and opponents can't

Choose taxing pieces your deck sidesteps: Winter Orb / Static Orb / Storage Matrix with an artifact-untapping commander (Meria), Collector Ouphe when your engine is the commander's ability, rule-of-law and anti-free-spell pieces when your own wins are activated abilities (Ratadrabik), Damping Sphere against combo, Cursed Totem when you don't rely on creature activations, Dauthi Voidwalker / Tinybones in any black list ('never not in a budget black list').

*Seen in:* Meria, Ratadrabik, Hapatra, Tannuk, Vren

## 11. Land count follows the plan, not a template

Ranges run from ~16-22 (dork-heavy or Codie turbo) through 29-34 to 39 (Tannuk landfall after four failed versions). Utility lands count as spells: Cephalid Coliseum, Bridgeworks Battle, Hydroelectric Specimen (an MDFC counter-creature), Rogue's Passage / Access Tunnel for evasion, Fell the Profane and Sink into Stupor MDFCs. Budget fixing uses pain lands, fast lands, slow / snarl lands, horizon and filter lands, Exotic Orchard and Command Tower. Few or no taplands.

*Seen in:* Tannuk, Gateway to Glory (Codie), Hapatra, Kefka

## 12. Upgrade by 1-for-1 quality bumps, not one pricey bomb

With extra budget, raise the quality of many cards one at a time (Negate -> Mana Drain) rather than adding a single $40 card. Ten better cards beat one bomb. A synergy deck is not a pile of best cards, so check each swap against the deck's jobs.

*Seen in:* Ratadrabik

## 13. Move the game to instant speed where the commander rewards it

If the commander triggers on every end step or every turn (Vren), add flash enablers and instant edicts so the engine runs on opponents' turns too (Final-Word Phantom is the cheapest). Save instant-speed ramp for opponents' turns when it triggers the commander (Tannuk).

*Seen in:* Vren, Tannuk, Alaundo

## 14. Underplayed commanders with broken text

He favors commanders that read badly or sit outside EDHREC's top lists (Meria, Ratadrabik, Tannuk, Hapatra) because opponents underrate them and budget cards fill out the engine. Look for pushed uncommons and new-set commanders that have no expensive staple shell yet.

*Seen in:* Meria, Ratadrabik, Tannuk, Hapatra

## Rules of thumb

- Average mana value of nonland cards around 2.0-2.6 in every flagship list (Kefka 2.0, Hapatra 2.19, Vren 2.36, Tannuk 2.61).
- Interaction counts of 20-25 in control/combo-control shells. Counterspells dominate in blue, plus 1-mana removal.
- Tutors: 6-12 in combo lists, 0 in Vren (by design for Bracket 3).
- Utility lands and MDFC spell-lands count toward both lands and spells.
- Price snapshots go stale. Re-price ($100 target) before buying any list.
