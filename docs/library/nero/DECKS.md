## Tannuk — Gruul landfall midrange/control combo

[$100 Tannuk - High Power -Woodlands Ablaze🌳🔥](https://moxfield.com/decks/r4TAMkoMBkWnDtJIZvYW4A)

**Plan:** Play two lands every turn possible (Tannuk draws on the second and burns on each). Lots of instant-speed land drops so it also runs on opponents' turns. Tannuk is a value engine and combo outlet, not a turbo or token commander: cast it turns 2-4 with enough lands to start chaining. 39 lands, heavy interaction, wins around turns 5-7.

**Ideal turn:** Tannuk turns 2-4, win attempt turns 5-7.

**Mulligan:** Lands plus land ramp. Tannuk need not come down early.

**Cuts:** Version 5. Earlier versions split themes (generic landfall tokens, or too many 1-mana dorks for a turbo Tannuk) and felt clunky. He rebuilt from scratch around 39 lands.

**Win lines:**

- Tannuk, Memorial Ensign + Ashaya, Soul of the Wild + Quirion Ranger
- Tannuk, Memorial Ensign + Ashaya, Soul of the Wild + Petravark
- Tannuk, Memorial Ensign + Kodama of the East Tree + Tireless Tracker + Gruul Turf
- Tannuk, Memorial Ensign + Kodama of the East Tree + Tireless Provisioner + Arid Archway
- Tannuk, Memorial Ensign + Ashaya, Soul of the Wild + Titania, Protector of Argoth + Vesuva
- Shifting Woodland + Aftermath Analyst + Sylvan Safekeeper + Spelunking
- Shifting Woodland + Aftermath Analyst + Zuran Orb + Lotus Cobra
- Valakut Exploration + Ashaya, Soul of the Wild + Quirion Ranger

**How the lines work:**

- Tannuk, Valakut Exploration or Sabotender are interchangeable burn outlets in every line.
- Ashaya makes creatures Forests: Quirion Ranger returns itself to untap a creature-land, then recast it. Infinite landfall. Petravark exiles itself as a land and returns, infinitely.
- Kodama + a Tireless bro + a bounce land (Gruul Turf/Arid Archway) loops the bounce land.
- Ashaya + Titania + Vesuva: Vesuva copies Titania (a land), the legend rule kills one, Titania returns Vesuva. Infinite 5/3s even without Tannuk.
- Shifting Woodland copies a binned Aftermath Analyst. With a sac outlet (Sylvan Safekeeper/Zuran Orb) and untapped-land enablers (Spelunking/Horizon Explorer/Stone-Seeder) or mana-per-land (Lotus Cobra/Provisioner), it loops for infinite mana and landfall.

**Key cards:** Archdruid's Charm, Sylvan Safekeeper, Collector Ouphe, Return the Favor, Ricochet Trap, Keen Sense, Snake Umbra, Horizon Explorer, Spelunking, Shared Summons, Worldsoul's Rage, Stump Stomp // Burnwillow Clearing, Bridgeworks Battle // Tanglespan Bridgeworks

**Would add with more budget:** Lumra, Bellow of the Woods

## Hapatra — Golgari aggro-tempo combo (-1/-1 counters)

[$100 Hapatra - High Power - Complete Lockdown ❌](https://moxfield.com/decks/RlxkDf4X9ke1Rh81SFI5Dg)

**Plan:** Hapatra on turn 2 (turn 1 with Elvish Spirit Guide). Snipe mana dorks and early plays with -1/-1 counters while every counter makes a deathtouch Snake that feeds Skullclamp, Toski and Braids. Plays like Yuriko-style tempo that pivots into a sacrifice-loop combo. Can win turns 3-4 if ignored.

**Ideal turn:** Hapatra turn 2, lock the board turns 3-4, combo when ready.

**Mulligan:** Hapatra on turn 2 plus a way to connect (evasion/removal), or a strong engine piece.

**Cuts:** Cut the Chain of Smog + Witherbloom Apprentice backup combo because those cards were dead outside the combo.

**Win lines:**

- Hapatra, Vizier of Poisons + Yawgmoth, Thran Physician + Blood Artist + Young Wolf
- Hapatra, Vizier of Poisons + Yawgmoth, Thran Physician + Zulaport Cutthroat + Young Wolf
- Nest of Scarabs + Yawgmoth, Thran Physician + Blood Artist + Young Wolf
- Blowfly Infestation + Nest of Scarabs + Blood Artist
- Blowfly Infestation + Nest of Scarabs + Zulaport Cutthroat
- Hapatra, Vizier of Poisons + Ivy Lane Denizen + Devoted Druid
- Ivy Lane Denizen + Scurry Oak
- Devoted Druid + Hazel's Brewmaster + Genesis Wave
- Devoted Druid + Ivy Lane Denizen + Hapatra, Vizier of Poisons + Genesis Wave

**How the lines work:**

- Hapatra + Yawgmoth is the core engine even without a payoff: it wipes X/1s and draws through the deck. Default tutor target when you lack a full win.
- Yawgmoth puts -1/-1 on Young Wolf, sacrificing a Snake. Hapatra replaces the Snake and Wolf returns with +1/+1 (undying). Blood Artist/Zulaport/Obelisk Spider drain each time. Repeat.
- Blowfly + Nest of Scarabs: a -1/-1 death makes an Insect and Blowfly moves a counter to kill it, so it loops with any drain payoff.
- Devoted Druid + Ivy Lane Denizen + Hapatra makes infinite green mana. Sink it into Genesis Wave to dump the deck.
- Hazel's Brewmaster exiling a dead Devoted Druid gives Food that taps for G and untaps (infinite mana with Druid's ability), so it's a combo starter and backup Yawgmoth.

**Key cards:** Yawgmoth, Thran Physician, Skullclamp, Conqueror's Flail, Destiny Spinner, Dauthi Voidwalker, Viridian Longbow, Archdruid's Charm, Generous Patron, Black Sun's Zenith, Shared Summons, Skeleton Key, Call of the Ring, Canopy Cover

## Vren — Dimir creature-stax control (no tutors, no hard combos)

[$100 Vren - High Power - MetaBuster Brew👊](https://moxfield.com/decks/RPmJnNiKaU-zuGafZ2J9Tg)

**Plan:** Built as 'Removal.exe', not Rat tribal: edict creatures, instant edicts and one-sided wipes exile opposing creatures, and each exiled creature makes a growing Rat at EVERY end step. Ward 2 keeps Vren alive, its exile effect hoses graveyard decks, and recursion loops the edict creatures. Built for Bracket 3 and low Bracket 4.

**Ideal turn:** Vren turn 2-3 (Dark Ritual helps), lock the board from turn 3.

**Mulligan:** Vren early plus removal density.

**How the lines work:**

- No hard combos by design. Soft locks: Narset, Parter of Veils or Notion Thief + Windfall/Whispering Madness empties opponents' hands; add Dauthi Voidwalker to cast their discards.
- Instant-speed edicts on opponents' turns still make Rats at that end step, so you swing for lethal on your own turn.

**Key cards:** Accursed Marauder, Plaguecrafter, Merciless Executioner, Crippling Fear, Sheoldred's Edict, Liliana's Triumph, Soul Shatter, Phyrexian Reclamation, Chthonian Nightmare, Altar of Bhaal // Bone Offering, Grazilaxx, Illithid Scholar, Braids, Arisen Nightmare, Enduring Curiosity, Idol of Oblivion, Force of Despair

## Kefka (2026 update) — Grixis combo-control

[$100 Kefka - High Power - 🖤2026 Update🖤](https://moxfield.com/decks/gq3LX9pvc0yPxz-HadNvSA)

**Plan:** Kefka on turn 3 rips hands and draws 2-4 per trigger (each ETB, flicker, clone or attack). Opponents face a lose-lose: discard similar types (giving you information) or discard freely (giving you cards). Maximize triggers with Harmonic Prodigy, Gogo, Combat Celebrant and Rionya, protect Kefka with cheap counters and blink, then win with infinite hasty tokens or infinite combats.

**Ideal turn:** Kefka turn 3 (T1 ramp + T2 ramp, or any 2-mana producer). Wins mid-game via combo.

**Mulligan:** Keep 2-3 lands + T1 ramp + T2 ramp, or 2-3 lands + a 2-mana generator. Otherwise mulligan (0-3 mulls is normal).

**Win lines:**

- Rionya, Fire Dancer + Combat Celebrant
- Rionya, Fire Dancer + Fear of Missing Out
- Dualcaster Mage + Electroduplicate
- Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame
- Dualcaster Mage + Electroduplicate + Mayhem Devil
- Dualcaster Mage + Ghostly Flicker + Kefka, Court Mage // Kefka, Ruler of Ruin

**How the lines work:**

- Rionya + Combat Celebrant/FOMO: Rionya's start-of-combat copy exerts for an extra combat, repeat. Against first-strike/indestructible blockers, cast an instant/sorcery first so two copies attack.
- Dualcaster + Electroduplicate/Twinflame: cast the copy spell on your creature and hold priority with Dualcaster; it copies the spell onto itself, so you get infinite hasty copies.
- With Mayhem Devil out, run the Dualcaster loop and the end-step sacrifice of the copies burns the table.
- Dualcaster + Ghostly Flicker (target Dualcaster + a land) makes infinite mana, then flicker Kefka repeatedly to strip hands and tutor any other line.

**Key cards:** Harmonic Prodigy, Generator Servant, Essence Flux, Ghostly Flicker, Snapback, Mayhem Devil, Cephalid Coliseum, Demonic Counsel, Lively Dirge, Splinter's Technique

**Would add with more budget:** Displacer Kitten

## Ratadrabik — Orzhov stax-control with Ring-tempt combos

**Mulligan:** Mulligan toward solid turn 1-3 plays or a relevant stax piece.

**Win lines:**

- Ratadrabik of Urborg + Boromir, Warden of the Tower + Altar of the Brood
- Ratadrabik of Urborg + Boromir, Warden of the Tower + Vengeful Bloodwitch
- Ratadrabik of Urborg + Boromir, Warden of the Tower + Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim

- Ratadrabik of Urborg + Samwise the Stouthearted + Altar of Dementia
- Ratadrabik of Urborg + Gollum, Patient Plotter + Blasting Station
- Ratadrabik of Urborg + Boromir, Warden of the Tower + Bartolomé del Presidio
- Abdel Adrian, Gorion's Ward + Animate Dead + Altar of the Brood
- Leonin Relic-Warder + Necromancy + Vengeful Bloodwitch

**How the lines work:**

- Sacrifice Boromir and Ratadrabik makes a token copy. Order triggers so Rata resolves first, then Boromir's tempt makes the token legendary again. Any death/ETB payoff repeats.
